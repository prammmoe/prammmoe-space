# E-Voucher Audit

## Findings

1. **HIGH — Matcha identity is exposed before the intended reveal** (R-01, R-06)
   Landing copy, green palette, steam marks, and “free matcha” labels reveal the gift before an envelope is opened.
2. **HIGH — Envelope click skips the opening moment** (R-19, R-26)
   The flap changes only on hover/focus, while click immediately opens the voucher dialog instead of confirming the user’s action with a purposeful opening sequence.
3. **MEDIUM — Decorative stars and direction arrows compete with the secret-letter premise** (R-04, R-08)
   Repeated generic marks add visual noise without communicating a real action or state.

## Approved scope

The user approved all findings: use a secret-letter landing experience, reserve the supplied voucher artwork as the reveal, and add one click-triggered envelope-opening animation with a reduced-motion bypass.
