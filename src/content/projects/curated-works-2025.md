## Selected work in iOS engineering

### [ERHA Buddy: Production iOS Engineering](/projects/erha-buddy/)

Developing and maintaining a production app at Arya Noble: guest shopping and registration, Flash Sale, live campaign updates, request signing, App Attest, order calculation, server-issued checkout quotes, Home, and Referral.

### [SLNG: A Native Indonesian Slang Translator](/projects/slng/)

A translator for foreign students that captures slang meaning and social context through a contextual data model, native interactions, multiple entry points, voice input, and a Go backend. The case study includes the App Store metrics recorded in the portfolio.

### [Paintee: Face-Aware AR Guidance](/projects/paintee/)

Helping beginners create proportional, symmetrical face painting through a continuous AR session and voice-guided application.

Contributing dynamic lesson routing, generic content structures, and reusable views to a mindfulness learning experience.
